Acceptable use

Using WHITE responsibly

These rules apply to everyone using this installation of WHITE.

You may

  • Assess websites that you or your organisation own.
  • Assess websites for which you have explicit, preferably written, permission from the owner.
  • Share WHITE reports with people who need them to improve the assessed website.

You must not

  • Scan websites you do not own and are not authorized to assess.
  • Attempt to bypass WHITE's ownership verification, rate limits or network protections.
  • Use WHITE, or its reports, to attack, disrupt or gain unauthorized access to any system.
  • Present WHITE results as a guarantee or certification of security.

Why this matters

Assessing a website without permission can be unlawful in many countries, even when the checks are gentle. WHITE is designed to help owners understand and improve their own websites.
