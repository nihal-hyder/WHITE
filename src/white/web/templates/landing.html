Responsible by default

What WHITE will never do.

An assessment tool should never become a risk itself. These limits are enforced in the software, not just promised.

✕Scan without permissionEvery scan requires confirmation, and accounts must verify domain ownership.
✕Attempt to break inNo password guessing, exploitation, denial-of-service testing or form submission.
✕Reach internal networksPrivate, local and cloud-internal addresses are refused at the connection level.
✕Store your secretsCookie values, tokens and passwords are redacted from every report.
✕Overstate the results"Passed" means only that a specific issue wasn't detected. No fake scores.
✕Share your dataYour scans are visible only to your account, enforced by the database itself.
